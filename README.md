# GPS-photo-video-Camera-Map-Tag App
**Privacy Policy - GPS Photo Video Camera Map Tag**
Effective date: 13.11.2026

This policy explains how the app "GPS Photo Video Camera Map Tag" (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by KJVJ Novel Tech ("we", "us"). Contact: kjvjnoveltechnologies@gmail.com

1. Summary in plain words
The App has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your photos and videos stay on your phone. The App uses your location, camera and microphone only to do what you ask. To find the village name and to show a map picture, the App contacts the outside services listed below. The App shows ads from Google AdMob.

2. Information the App uses
Camera and microphone: used to take photos and record videos. They are processed on your phone and saved to your gallery (folder DCIM/Camera). We never receive them.
Precise location (GPS), altitude and compass sensors: used only while the App is open, to print the position, accuracy, altitude and direction on your photos and videos. For photos, the position is also written into the photo's EXIF data.
Village name and PIN code: to find them, the App sends your coordinates to a place-name service. This is OpenStreetMap Nominatim, or another service of the same kind (for example LocationIQ) if we switch, and as a backup the geocoder that is built into your Android phone (provided by Google).
Map picture: the App downloads satellite map pieces from Esri. The request names the map tile of your area, so Esri can tell your approximate location.
These services also receive your IP address and technical details such as the type of phone, as any internet request does. They handle that information under their own privacy policies (see section 8).
Your name or note: the name you type in Settings is stored only on your phone and is printed on your photos and videos.
Saved place names: to avoid asking again, the App keeps a small list of recent place names on your phone. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

3. Advertising
The App shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, app usage information and diagnostics. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the App first asks for your consent. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings under Privacy > Ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites for how Google uses data from apps that use its ads.

4. What we do not do
We do not create user accounts, sell your information, upload your photos, videos, audio or location to our own servers, or track you across apps.

5. Sharing
Information goes only to the service providers named in this policy (place-name service, map provider and Google for ads), and only to provide those features.

6. Keeping and deleting information
Everything the App stores is on your phone. You can delete photos and videos from your gallery at any time, and you can remove the App's settings and saved place names by clearing its data or uninstalling it. Information held by the outside services is kept under their own policies.

7. Children
The App is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Third-party privacy policies
Google (AdMob, Android geocoder): https://policies.google.com/privacy
OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim): https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
Esri (map imagery): https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview
LocationIQ (only if we use it): https://locationiq.com/privacy

9. Security
The App sends requests to the services above over secure (HTTPS) connections.

10. Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. If you have a question or a complaint, or you want to exercise any right you have under the laws that apply to you (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), write to kjvjnoveltechnologies@gmail.com and we will reply.

11. Changes to this policy
If we add features (for example notifications or a paid subscription) we will update this policy and change the effective date above.
